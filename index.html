Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Panic handles information when you use the Pocket Panic mobile game.

Pocket Panic is developed by Abdul Raffay. This policy currently applies to the Android version of the game.

1. About Pocket Panic

Pocket Panic is an offline-first casual mobile game intended for adults aged 18 and older. The game does not require users to create an account, register, log in, or provide personal profile information.

The game does not operate its own user account system, backend service, or cloud-save service.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Pocket Panic stores a limited amount of information locally on your device to provide and maintain basic game functionality. This may include:

  • Your best score.
  • Your sound preference.
  • Advertising counters and the timing of previously displayed interstitial ads.
  • Banner layout measurements used by the game.
  • A temporary indicator related to an unfinished consent form.

This information is not submitted to a Pocket Panic server because the game does not operate its own backend service.

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the app's storage through your Android device settings or uninstalling the game. Uninstalling or clearing app storage may remove your saved game progress and preferences.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Pocket Panic uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google acts as a third-party service provider and may collect and process certain information through its advertising SDK.

Depending on the SDK's operation and applicable settings, information processed by Google may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions and video views.
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and related technical details.
  • Device identifiers, including App Set ID and other identifiers where applicable.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, stability and performance data.
  • Advertising consent information and related privacy signals.

The Android Advertising ID permission is removed in the current Android app configuration. This does not mean that all other device identifiers or technical information are unavailable to advertising SDKs.

Pocket Panic requests non-personalized advertisements. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no information is processed. Google may still process information for purposes such as delivering ads, measuring advertising performance, limiting repeated ads, maintaining security and preventing fraud.

Google handles information collected through its services under its own privacy practices. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Pocket Panic uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine whether a privacy or consent message is required in your region.

Where applicable, Google may present a consent message or other privacy choices. The game uses the available consent information to determine whether advertising requests may be made.

Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, the game provides access to the relevant privacy options through its Settings screen. This entry point may not be displayed when Google determines that it is not required.

Consent requirements and available choices may differ depending on your location and applicable privacy regulations. Google's consent service may communicate with Google to retrieve or update consent information.

5. How Advertisements Are Displayed

The game may display banner advertisements on selected non-gameplay screens and interstitial advertisements between completed game runs. Interstitial advertisements are not intended to interrupt active gameplay.

Advertising availability depends on Google's services, regional availability, consent requirements, and advertising inventory. Advertisements may not be available in every country or on every occasion.

6. Information Sharing

Abdul Raffay does not operate a Pocket Panic backend and does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be collected or processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads and UMP SDKs as described in this policy. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own privacy terms and policies.

7. Data Security and Backups

Information transmitted by Google's SDKs is subject to the security measures described in Google's documentation, including encryption in transit where specified by Google.

Pocket Panic's Android configuration disables the app's standard Android cloud backup. However, this setting should not be understood as a guarantee that every form of device-to-device transfer or third-party SDK data handling is prevented.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game information stored locally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game, subject to Android device behavior and any applicable transfer mechanisms.

Google may retain information collected through its advertising and consent services according to Google's own retention policies. Abdul Raffay does not control Google's independent retention practices.

For information about Google's data practices or available privacy controls, please refer to Google's privacy policy.

9. Children's Privacy

Pocket Panic is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed or marketed for children. The game does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its advertising services, or applicable requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
